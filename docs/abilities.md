# Abilities

## Emerald Katana

### Bushwalk & Meld

Allows wielder to walk through leaves and makes them invisible while doing so.

### Throw Seeds

Throws seeds forward with high velocity. 
Any living entity hit by flying seeds will be hit by a small amount and poisoned.

### Sprout

Is an effect that accompanies poison any time wielder applies it.

When a poisoned living entity dies, spreads grass and grows plants around itself.
Always applied on the wielder.

### Coalescence

Applies regeneration when near grass or flowers.
Regeneration level is based on amount of grass and flowers nearby.
